Our Target: H&M

As one of the top global fashion retailers, H&M plays a major role in driving overproduction and waste. Despite their "Conscious Collection" branding, the company continues to contribute to harmful practices. Our campaign demands:

  • Full transparency in supply chains.
  • Reduction in overproduction and waste.
  • Commitment to living wages for all garment workers.

Why This Matters

The fashion industry produces more than 100 billion garments a year—most of which end up in landfills. Our campaign is about rethinking fashion: from fast to fair, from wasteful to responsible.
