How To Get Started

My guitar actually came with tutotial book. It taught me a few chords and some easy populat songs as well like Twinkle, Twinkle and Happy Birthday. If you're able to purchase a deal like that, I recommend it. Since it'll be your first guitar, there really is no point in you getting one that absurdly expensive. It's the guitar you getting to know if it's something you'd like to continue with and the one you'll make many mistakes with, so don't get oee that'll make you refret the purchase or one you'll be too afraid to tweak.

I would also recommend youtube videos. I tried using apps, but it seems like the really good ones hide behind paywalls, and this was not a hobby I was ready to pay for. So I watched a bunch of youtube videos that taight more chords and melodies and others that teach you haw to play different songs. I would also advise to take it slow. Just becuase it's a song you like, it doesn't mean you should learn it. Some songs are harder to learn than others, so start easy and work your way up.

Why I Love the Guitar

I'm rather indecisive about everything that I do. It gets to the point where I overthink something and eventually just end up not doing anything. But one of the few things I didn't overthink was getting a guitar. It's why I love it so much. As one of the decisions I was able to go through with, it has a very special place in my heart.

I'm not very good and I didn't even buy it with my own money (if I had, then I'd definitely have to keep at it), but I keep coming back to it. And despite my slow learning, I'm still having a lot of fun. I still love to play, it hasn't gotten old yet and I doubt it will.
