Why I Love the Guitar

I'm rather indecisive about everything that I do. It gets to the point where I overthink something and eventually just end up not doing anything. But one of the few things I didn't overthink was getting a guitar. It's why I love it so much. As one of the decisions I was able to go through with, it has a very special place in my heart.

I'm not very good and I didn't even buy it with my own money (if I had, then I'd definitely have to keep at it), but I keep coming back to it. And despite my slow learning, I'm still having a lot of fun. I still love to play, it hasn't gotten old yet and I doubt it will.
